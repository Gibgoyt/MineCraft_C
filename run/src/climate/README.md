# run/src/climate

**Purpose:** the climate of plan 004, worked out strictly top to bottom (master plan section 3). Latitude from y alone (master plan 5.1, phase 02); its part `sun/` gives where the sun is. From phase 03, the **climate cells**: every 64 by 64 block cell's latitude, mean ground height, whether it is sea, a large lake or land, how far and which way the nearest connected sea is, how far and which way the nearest land is from a sea cell, and its continentality κ. Cells are worked out a tile (16 by 16 cells, 1024 by 1024 blocks) at a time by background jobs, nearest the player first, kept in a cache, never saved: they come back from the seed. From phase 04, each cell also has its **ocean current** (warm, cold or neutral, its strength and, through `climate_current_flow`, its flow), its seasonal swing and lag, and its **heat-low pressure** and **wind** for any day, stored as a yearly average plus one yearly wave (`air/`, `harmonic/`, with the knot tables in `normals/`). From phase 05, each cell also has its **normals**: the yearly mean temperature at sea level (`heat/`), rain on any day as an average and two yearly waves (`rain/`), and the labels worked out from them, hurricane season, the hurricane-coast and monsoon flags, the snow balance height and the climate class (`season/`); `climate_normals_at` reads them all at any block, height and day.
**Tier:** Owner. It reserves one `heap_region_t`, `CLIMATE_REGION_BYTES` (146 MiB of address space, only touched pages cost memory): the state at its start, the warp bank's fields, the normals' curves (phase 04 and 05: `arena_normals`, 4 KiB at `CLIMATE_NORMALS_OFFSET`, built once in `climate_init`), the two tables' slots, the tiles' cells (128 MiB) and the mask pages (16 MiB). Nothing is allocated after `climate_init`.
**Owns:** that region, through `climate_t.owned_region`; `climate_t` is 32 bytes, in `app_t`, because main's frame holds `app_t` and sits at the 16 KiB limit, so everything else, `climate_state_t` included, is in the region.
**Lifetime:** Process for the region (`climate_init`, `climate_deinit` in `app_init`, `app_deinit`). World for what is in it: `climate_open` binds a terrain, seeds the warp and empties both tables; `climate_close` waits for its jobs and forgets the world.
**Threads:** latitude, `climate_cell_get`, `climate_sample_at`, `climate_area_ready` and `climate_warp_at` from any thread. `climate_schedule` from the sim, once a step. `climate_prepare_area`, `climate_prepare_begin`, `climate_prepare_step`, `climate_prepare_tile` and `climate_close` from any thread but a worker; a begun precompute is stepped by the thread that began it. `climate_init`, `climate_deinit`, `climate_open` and `climate_open_ground` with nothing else touching it. Tile and page jobs run on the workers; a job never waits.
**Public functions:**

- `climate_latitude_deg`: `phi(y) = (180 / pi) asin(sin(pi y / 2Y))`, in degrees, positive north, with `Y = CLIMATE_POLE_Y = 10000 * 90 / 23.5 = 38297.87` blocks. A triangle wave: 0 at y = 0, 23.5 at the tropic (10,000), 66.5 at the polar circle (28,298), 90 at the pole (38,298), 0 again at 76,596, and -90 at -38,298. y is first brought into one period, `CLIMATE_LATITUDE_PERIOD = 4Y`, so a y out at the world's edge keeps its digits. x plays no part.
- `climate_init`, `climate_deinit`: the region, a pair.
- `climate_open`, `climate_open_ground`, `climate_close`, `climate_is_open`: a world. `climate_open` takes the terrain, and its ground is `terrain_height_estimate_area`; `climate_open_ground` takes any pure ground function, for tests and the bench, so real and synthetic worlds go down one code path.
- `climate_schedule`: the sim's, each step. Moves the keep square to the player and queues up to `CLIMATE_JOBS_IN_FLIGHT_MAX` (4) tiles not yet built within `radius_blocks` plus the read reach (96), nearest the player's block to the tile's centre first, ties by y then x. A full shared queue undoes the claim, for next step. Never waits.
- `climate_area_ready`: whether every tile a read anywhere in a block rectangle touches is built: the gate that holds a column back until its tiles are there.
- `climate_prepare_area`: builds all of a rectangle's tiles on all workers and waits: mask pages first, a job each, so no two jobs build one page, then the tiles. Tells a progress callback as jobs finish. It is `climate_prepare_begin` and then `climate_prepare_step` until true.
- `climate_prepare_begin`, `climate_prepare_step`: the same a round at a time, for a thread that draws between steps: begin queues the pages' jobs and returns; each step says how many jobs are done of how many, queues the tiles' jobs once the pages' are done, and never waits for a job still running. A new world begins it over spawn ± (render distance × 16 + 2048), and the loading screen steps it once a frame, showing the percent, before play.
- `climate_prepare_tile`: one tile in one job, its window's missing pages built inside it: what a tile costs, for the bench and the tests.
- `climate_cell_get`: the cell holding a block, copied out under the read protocol.
- `climate_sample_at`: the warped bilinear read. The point is moved by two warp fields, `"climate_cells/warp_x"` and `"climate_cells/warp_y"` (2D, 1/256 cycles a block, 2 octaves, spread at 0.35), up to 32 blocks each way; then the four cells round it are blended. Latitude is never blended: it comes from the exact y, so a read at the pole says 90.
- `climate_warp_at`, `climate_warp_set`: where a read is taken from, and a test's hook to turn the warp off.
- `climate_air_at` (phase 04): air at a block and a day: the six wind and three heat-low coefficients and `C × strength / 255` blended between the same four cells, at the same warped point, as `climate_sample_at`, then the waves evaluated (both are linear, so blending coefficients is blending the day's values); the belt pressure `p_belt(ψ)` at the exact y and day; the current's kind and strength from the nearest cell, never blended. False, writing nothing, if a tile is not built.
- `climate_sea_temperature` (phase 04): master plan 6.3's `T_sea = T̄_lat(φ) + C × strength / 255 + A_sea(φ) s_h cos(2π(d - 215) / 365)` for a cell.
- `climate_current_flow` (phase 04): a current's flow along its coast, warm toward the pole, cold toward the equator; a land cell's coast is taken as `-sea_direction`.
- `climate_normals`: the curves `climate_init` built.
- `climate_normals_at` (phase 05): a place's normals on a day, as a `climate_normals_at_t` (the phase 05 file's `climate_normals_t`, renamed because phase 04's knot curves hold that name): `t_mean`, `t_swing`, `t_lag_days`, κ, the ground, the five rain numbers and `snow_balance_z` (over the corners that are not sea only, whose 9999 means "no height") blended between the same four cells at the same warped point, then evaluated: the temperature at any `z` (the player's height for the HUD; NaN for the blended ground, for maps), with `B` below 0 when the nearest cell is land, the daily range, high and low, the warmest and coldest month, rain on the day and over the year, the wettest day, the snow share; the class, flags, today's hurricane season and monsoon from the nearest cell, and for a sea cell its sea temperature and `s_hur`. False, writing nothing, if a tile is not built.
- `climate_test_hooks_set` (phase 05): tests only. A fixed wind, a fixed `P_belt`, frozen seasons, `O = 1`, for every tile built after; `climate_open` puts them back to all off.

**A tile job**, in order: its window from 17 by 17 mask pages (read, or claimed and built, or built privately), then `climate_cell_build_tile` (`cell/`), which hands its window out as a `climate_window_t`, then `climate_air_build_tile` (`air/`) on that window, then phase 05's `climate_heat_build_tile`, `climate_rain_build_tile` and `climate_season_build_tile` (`heat/`, `rain/`, `season/`) on the same window and cells, in that order, then publish. Each tile is built by exactly one job, in a fixed order, so the bytes are the same on 1 worker or 16.

**The sea rule in an infinite world:** each tile decides inside its own window, the tile plus 8 km each way (17 by 17 tiles). Water whose body reaches the sea floor's depth (`CLIMATE_SEA_FLOOR_BLOCKS`, -30) anywhere in the window, or covers over 33.6 km² inside it, is sea (touching the window's edge, the plan's first rule, left steps at tile borders: the phase 03 file's Issues found 17); enclosed water over 2 km² is a large lake; smaller lakes are land for every climate purpose. A tile's cells depend only on its window, so on nothing else that has been worked out. Distances are capped at 8192 blocks, which the 8 km margin covers.

**May be called by:** `main`, which holds the `climate_t` and starts and stops it; `game`, which borrows it, schedules tiles, gates columns and prepares a new world's spawn; `mob` and `player` for latitude (`compile/src/standards_lint/rules_include/index.c` ranks it 8, under `render`); later climate parts. `render` is never given it: it is handed a sun direction as three floats.
**Depends on:** its parts `sea/`, `cache/`, `mask/`, `cell/`, `normals/`, `harmonic/`, `air/`, `heat/`, `rain/`, `season/`, `layer/` and `sun/`; `terrain` (the height estimate and the noise bank, nothing else), `job`, `heap`, `arena`, `time`, `log`, `assert`, `types`, glibc's libm.

Positive y is north and positive x is east (Ahmed, 2026-11-04). The period is 153,191.49 blocks, not a whole number, so the tropic stays at exactly 10,000: shifting y by the whole 153,191 moves latitude by about 0.001 degrees (phase 02 file, Issues found 2).

Everything about the cells, with worked examples, is in `plans/004-ahmed-bohemoth-climate-system-phase-03-height-estimate-and-climate-cells.md`; currents, pressure and winds in `plans/004-ahmed-bohemoth-climate-system-phase-04-currents-pressure-and-winds.md`; temperature, rain and storm seasons in `plans/004-ahmed-bohemoth-climate-system-phase-05-temperature-rain-and-storm-seasons.md`.
