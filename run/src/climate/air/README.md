# run/src/climate/air

**Purpose:** the movement of water and air, plan 004 master plan 6.3 to 6.5 and 6.9, by the phase 04 file. For one tile, from its window (`climate_window_t`, built by `climate/cell`): every cell's **ocean current** (warm, cold or neutral by the table of latitude and coast side, its strength fading over 600 blocks offshore, a land cell taking its nearest sea's as `e^(-d_s / 600)`), its seasonal **swing and lag** (`t_swing`, `t_lag_days`, master plan 6.6, needed by heat lows), its **heat low or cold high** `Δp_heat = -0.6 κ (T_season - T̄)` and its **wind**: the belt wind by the wind latitude `ψ = φ - 0.5 δ(d - 30)` (trades, westerlies, polar easterlies, blended over 6° with a smoothstep, the doldrums and horse latitudes slower), plus the pressure wind `900 R_θ(-∇Δp)` turned 70° clockwise in the north and anticlockwise in the south, then steered along steep slopes and sped up with altitude. Wind and heat-low pressure are worked out on 12 days and stored as an average plus one yearly wave (`climate/harmonic`). Nothing flows and nothing is integrated over time (Ahmed: "physics must not be simulated").
**Tier:** Arena by depth (`run/src/climate/air/`, depth 2): `climate_air_build_tile` takes about 40 KiB of scratch from the arena it is given, the worker's Job arena. Everything else allocates nothing.
**Owns:** nothing.
**Lifetime:** none of its own; the scratch dies with the job.
**Threads:** any. Every function is pure: the same window gives the same bytes on any worker, every loop runs south row first, x fastest, and every sum adds in one order.
**Public functions:**

- `climate_air_build_tile`: fills `current`, `current_strength`, `t_swing`, `t_lag_days`, `wind[6]` and `pressure[3]` of a tile's 256 cells, whose phase 03 fields are set. The tile and a ring of one cell round it (18 by 18) get κ, swing and lag, as the gradients need the neighbours; the window reaches 8 km past the tile, so two tiles agree at their border. An optional `climate_air_trace_t` receives the 12 days' belt wind, pressure wind, final wind and heat low per cell, and its `steer_off` turns steering off: the tests' hooks.
- The pieces, each with a worked example in the phase file: `climate_air_mirror_sign` (`e(y) = sgn cos(π y / 2Y)`, -1 in the strips mirrored past a pole), `climate_air_poleward`, `climate_air_smoothstep`, `climate_air_doldrums`, `climate_air_horse`, `climate_air_wind_latitude`, `climate_air_belt_wind`, `climate_air_turn_deg` (`-70 sgn φ e`), `climate_air_rotate`, `climate_air_pressure_wind`, `climate_air_steer` (never faster), `climate_air_altitude_factor`, `climate_air_current_kind`, `climate_air_sea_strength`, `climate_air_land_strength`, `climate_air_current_c`, `climate_air_flow` (warm toward the pole, cold toward the equator), `climate_air_swing`, `climate_air_lag_days`, `climate_air_heat_low`, `climate_air_sea_temperature` (master plan 6.3).
- `climate_air_t`: what the Owner's `climate_air_at` gives at a block and a day.

**The mirror.** Latitude is a triangle wave in y, so the strip from a pole to the next equator is a mirror image of the strip below it. Every north-south part of a table, the pressure wind's sense of turning and "toward the pole" are multiplied by `e(y)`, so a mirrored strip is exactly a mirror image. West and east coasts do not change: x is never mirrored (the phase file's Issues found 4).

Phase 05 made one function public, `climate_air_window_current`: a window sea cell's current and strength, as its own tile stores them, for the water a rain walk meets and a coast's nearest sea in the hurricane season. The tile's own sea cells take theirs from it too, so there is one home.

**May be called by:** its parent `run/src/climate/` (the tile job and the reads), `run/src/climate/layer/` for the current kinds and their constants.
**Depends on:** its siblings `climate/cell` (the window, the distance and κ formulas), `climate/harmonic` (the fit), `climate/normals` (the knot tables), `climate/sea` (the kinds and `CLIMATE_SEA_FAR`), `climate/sun` (the declination); its parent for `climate_latitude_deg` and the pole's y; `arena`, `types`, `math/det` (every cosine, sine and e^x since plan 006 phase 02: they reach the wind, the rain and the current, which decide blocks), libm for exact functions only.
