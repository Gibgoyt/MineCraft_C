# run/src/climate/weather

**Purpose:** plan 004 phase 07's weather. Each day's weather at any place, as a pure function of the seed, the place and the day (master plan 7.4), and the **weekly state** of every kept 64-block cell, which the census works out once a week and the save keeps (7.5): last week's temperatures, rain, snow on the ground, soil water, runoff, ice, prevailing wind, the 7 day kinds and the flood index. Nothing reads the weekly state yet but the census report: it is for phase 08 and the economy plan.

## Daily weather

- **Temperature:** `T' = σ_T N₁(x/2500, y/2500, D/10) + T'_sys`, `σ_T = 2 + 3κ` (κ the cell's own), with `N₁` scaled to a measured spread of 1 and clamped to ±3 (the phase file's Issues found 2). A high adds `0.5 σ_T e^{−r²/R²}` at 12:00, plus in local summer (phase 05's `t_day` above the yearly mean) and minus in winter. The day's mean is phase 05's normal at the ground plus `T'`. Its range is phase 05's normal range, cut by 60% if a low's or hurricane's radius covers the place at 12:00.
- **Rain**, as the sum of 24 hourly totals, hour `h` at `t = D + (h + ½)/24` (06:30 to 05:30): the background `P(d)/7 · (1 − f_sys) · g`, `g = max(0, 1 + 0.9 N₂(x/2000, y/2000, D/3))`, falls evenly over the hours where `N₃(x/1500, y/1500, (24D + h)/6) > 0`; each weather system's rain at that moment times the place's wetness (phase 06's `climate_systems_wetness`); the hour's total times 0.2 under a high. The snow part by the hour's temperature, the day's mean plus `range/2 · cos(2π(clock − 15)/24)`. `f_sys` is phase 06's `climate_systems_rain_share_today`.
- **Wind:** phase 04's at 12:00 plus every system's circling wind then; the strongest hour is the same belt wind plus the systems' at each hour. Phase 04's wind is read once a day, at 12:00, and serves 06:00 too: it is a yearly wave, the same to well under a percent a quarter of a day apart.
- **The day's kind** by the table in `week/`, from the day's numbers, the place's (sea distance, cold current, desert, a valley's depth below its 8 neighbours) and last week's state (soil for fog, rain for dust).
- **Same sums, any list:** a day is worked out from views of a day list: the systems alive at each instant whose reach (3 R, or a front's length plus its showers) touches the points asked about. A field skips a system past its reach before adding anything, and lists are sorted by keys that do not depend on how they were found, so a view of the census's list round a block of tiles gives each cell the same bits as a list round that one cell: the census's daily totals are the sums of what `climate_weather_at` says hour by hour. Each view also keeps every system's circle of reach at its instant, and where a point is outside them all the fields are not asked at all: each would give exactly nothing (a rain of 0, a cut of 1, no wind), so the bits are the same. A census reads each cell's normals for its 7 days in one call (`climate_normals_days_at`, the Owner's), and the daily cycle comes from a table of the 24 cosines, the same bits as working each out.
- `climate_weather_at`: the weather at a place, height and moment (`t = D + f`, `climate_weather_time_of_clock`): temperature at that height with the daily cycle, the hour's rain rate, snow share, wind and pressure with the systems', and phase 08's cloud, thunder (the nearest cell within 3 r, its distance and id), fog (fog days, fading by 10:00) and dust (dust-storm days, by the wind).

The three noise fields, `"climate_weather/spell"`, `"climate_weather/rain_amount"` and `"climate_weather/rain_hours"`, are the repo's 3D noise, two octaves each, made from the world seed in `climate_open`. **Measured** raw standard deviations, over 100,000 points spread across ±200 km and 10,000 days (`tests/run/climate/weather`, `the_noises_have_a_spread_of_1`, which holds each within 3%): N₁ 0.2013, N₂ 0.2008, N₃ 0.2015 (means within 0.001 of 0). Each is divided by its own.

## The weekly state

- **The store:** a hash table of up to `CLIMATE_WEEK_TILES_MAX` (8192) tiles of 16 by 16 cells, `climate_week_tile_t` (16,448 bytes, saved as it is by `run/src/save/climate/`), with beside each, never saved, its side data: each cell's ground height and sea flag (phase 03), its long-run mean weekly runoff, the last full week's events and the hurricanes that crossed it. A tile is kept once a column in it is generated (the sim marks the cell as the column commits), and so is every tile within 2 tiles of one (`MARGIN`), for drainage. It only grows. Past 8192 a tile is not kept, which is logged once, and nothing kept is lost. While a census runs, marks wait in a list and are applied after.
- **Spin-up:** a tile first worked starts at the state its cells would have after 104 weekly updates on their normals alone (no systems, no noise), each at its week's middle day, ending at the week before the census; all 104 days' normals are read in one call. The long-run mean weekly runoff is the second year of the same from week 1, so it does not depend on the week.
- **A census week for a block** (`climate_week_block_run`, up to 2 by 2 tiles in one job, sharing one list of weather systems a day): side data and spin-up where missing; then each of the 7 days ending at the census's 06:00 for every cell, and `week/`'s update; or in fast mode (4.2, weeks past 52 late) the fast week, snow, soil and runoff from the week's middle day with the spell and amount noises and no systems. A job reads and writes only its own tiles.
- **Flow** (`climate_week_flow_run`, one job, when no block job runs): every kept cell into `flow/`'s D8 over the ground heights, with this week's runoff and the long-run means, and each cell's flood index.

**Tier:** Arena: allocates only from the arenas it is given, the climate's week arena (the store, the noise fields) and a worker's Job arena (a block's day lists and views, in a child arena reset each day).
**Owns:** nothing; the store's memory is the Owner's week region.
**Lifetime:** World: `climate_weather_open` at `climate_open`, `climate_weather_close` at `climate_close`.
**Threads:** daily weather from any thread. The store's marks, adds and pending list from the sim; a block job on a worker, touching only its tiles; the flow job on one worker while no block job runs; sorted reads with nothing being added.
**Public functions:** `climate_weather_open`, `climate_weather_close`; `climate_weather_time_of_clock`, `climate_weather_spell`, `climate_weather_amount`, `climate_weather_hours`, `climate_weather_day`, `climate_weather_at`; `climate_week_spinup`, `climate_week_fast`, `climate_week_store`, `climate_week_store_find`, `climate_week_store_add`, `climate_week_store_adopt` (a tile read straight into its slot, by the file), `climate_week_store_mark`, `climate_week_store_pending_apply`, `climate_week_store_sorted`, `climate_week_store_empty`, `climate_week_store_cell`, `climate_week_block_run`, `climate_week_flow_run`, `climate_week_first_snow`.
**May be called by:** the Owner `run/src/climate/`, `run/src/climate/outlook/`, `game` (the census and the marks), `save` (the file), phases 08 to 11, the tests and the bench.
**Depends on:** its parts `week/` and `flow/`; `climate/systems`, `climate/systems/field` and `climate/systems/name` (phase 06's lists, fields and names), `climate/air` (the current's kinds), `climate/season` (the climate classes), `climate/heat` (the snow share), the Owner's reads (`climate_normals_at`, `climate_normals_days_at`, `climate_air_at`, `climate_cell_get`), `terrain` (the noise and the hashes), `calendar`, `arena`, `time`, `log`, `assert`, `types`, libm.

Everything else, with worked examples, is in `plans/004-ahmed-bohemoth-climate-system-phase-07-daily-weather-weekly-state-census-and-outlook.md`.
