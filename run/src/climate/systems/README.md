# run/src/climate/systems

**Purpose:** plan 004 phase 06's **weather systems** (master plan 7.2 to 7.4): hurricanes, mid-latitude lows with their cold fronts, highs and thunderstorm cells. Nothing about air or water is simulated. Each kind has spawn squares (16 km for hurricanes and lows, 24 km for highs, 1 km for cells); for each square and day a hash (`terrain_rng_hash3` of the kind's seed, the square and the day) gives a Poisson count by the inverse CDF at the kind's largest rate, each candidate draws 12 numbers from its own stream in a fixed order, and **thinning** keeps it with probability `rate(start, time) / rate_max`, so the kept ones are exactly a Poisson process with 7.2's varying rate:

- **Hurricanes:** `0.3 s_hur` (`CLIMATE_HURRICANE_RATE`; 0.02 in the master plan), `s_hur` from phase 05's rule on the **track-time sea temperature** (`T̄_lat + A_sea s_h cos(2π(d - 215)/365)`, no cell's current, so no tile is needed), over sea (the mask at or below 0 at the point: `CLIMATE_HURRICANE_OPEN_SEA_BLOCKS` is 0). Phase 06 also wanted water 2048 blocks north, south, east and west, which the terrain's seas almost never have, so no hurricane formed on a real seed; the final check dropped that and set the rate so a warm coast square gets about 2.4 a season on the survey's 8 seeds (the phase 06 file, Issues found 22).
- **Lows:** `0.06 - 0.02 s_h cos(2π(d - 200)/365)` for `35° ≤ |ψ| ≤ 65°`: 0.08 in the local winter, 0.04 in summer.
- **Highs:** 0.03 for `20° ≤ |ψ| ≤ 45°`, and in a continental interior in the local winter (land at the point and 3000 blocks away on 8 sides, `20° ≤ |ψ| ≤ 70°`: Issues found 10).
- **Cells:** 7.2.1's `G 3 clamp((T_day - 18)/10) P̄/P̄_max (1 + 2 f_monsoon)` at each 1 km square's centre from `climate_normals_at`; two processes per square-day, afternoon cells (13:00 to 19:00) and front cells at 4 times the rate kept only on a cold front, and either kept with probability 0.2 under a high: `(1 + 4 f_front) 0.2^{f_high}` exactly. A cell moves in a straight line with phase 04's wind at its start at noon, capped at 15 blocks a second.

**Tracks** (7.3), Euler steps of a quarter of a day, reading only latitude, the season and the coarse land mask: hurricanes go west at 3500 blocks a day with a 15% poleward drift, turn over 2 days once `|ψ|` reaches their turn latitude (20° to 28°), then go east at 6000 with a 30% poleward drift, all times their own speed scale (0.85 to 1.15); they halve each day over land or sea under 26 °C and end when under 5 hPa after the first third of their life. Lows go east with a 15% poleward drift, highs east with a 10% equatorward drift. Poleward drift stops past 85°, equatorward drift inside 5°. Strength follows the life curve `g(τ)`. Positions between samples are interpolated (`climate_system_state_at`).

**The day list** (`climate_systems_day_build`): every system whose 3 R footprint comes within the asked radius at some moment of day D: every spawn square within the kind's reach (its longest track plus 3 R: 84.0, 63.5, 71.2 and 4.2 km), every day of its longest life, sorted by kind, spawn time, square and index (cells by spawn time, then id), so the bytes are the same on 1 worker or 16 and whichever day was asked first. Cells come after big systems, which their thinning reads.

**The rain a system brings** (the phase 06 file's Issues found 11 and 12, found by the 10-year test): its raw rain (`field/`) times the **wetness** of where it falls, the day's normal over 5 mm (`climate_systems_wetness`), so storms rain hard where it is usually wet and lightly where it is usually dry, and the long-run total follows the normal down to a test cell's scale. A cell's peak rain is also times its square-day's **rain scale**, `1/3 ÷ (max(hot, 0.2) × P̄/P̄_max × (1 + 2 f_monsoon))`: heat, the wet season and the monsoon make cells more often (7.2.1, unchanged), and each one that much lighter, so they are not counted twice. A low's shield, and its front with it, is times the mean low rate over the rate it was born at, so the winter's twice as many lows bring rain in step with the normal's own season. The background's share of the normal is `1 - f_sys` on days some system can rain there (`climate_systems_rain_share_today`: cells when the normal day is over 18 °C, lows within 5° of their band), and the whole normal on other days; in hurricane season `f_sys` is `0.25 s_hur` more (`CLIMATE_SYSTEM_RAIN_SHARE_HURRICANE`, the final check), as the hurricanes bring that share. The tuned rates, each with its measurement, are in the phase file and marked in the header: the cells' gain 0.205 (from 1, 0.22 until the final check), lows 0.18 in winter and 0.09 in summer (from 0.08 and 0.04), lows' drift 0.08 (from 0.15), hurricanes 0.3 (from 0.02; 0.034 behind phase 06's open-sea rule; master plan Q5), `f_sys` blended 0.3 to 0.5 between 25° and 50° and down to 0.4 between 60° and 70°.

**The mask**, as a build reads it: a page cache of 64 entries in the build's arena; a page inside the climate's keep square is read from the table or claimed and built there; outside it no page can be kept, so the one column is read from the ground (`climate_mask_point`), which is the page's value bit for bit. The same heights whichever way.

**Tier:** Arena by depth: the list, its scratch and its mask page cache come from the arena it is given.
**Owns:** nothing.
**Lifetime:** a list lives as long as the caller's arena: built once a day per area, read all day.
**Threads:** any. Reads the climate (read-only after `climate_open`) and the mask table under phase 03's protocol.
**Public functions:** `climate_systems_day_build`, `climate_systems_day_build_stats`, `climate_system_state_at`; the rates `climate_systems_hurricane_rate`, `climate_systems_low_rate`, `climate_systems_high_rate`, `climate_systems_thunder_rate`, `climate_systems_thunder_rate_scale`, `climate_systems_thunder_base`, `climate_systems_thunder_rain_scale`, `climate_systems_sea_temperature`, `climate_systems_hurricane_s`; `climate_systems_reach`, `climate_systems_square`; the pieces for tests and phase 07: `climate_systems_day_of_year`, `climate_systems_smoothstep`, `climate_systems_life`, `climate_systems_uniform`, `climate_systems_poisson`, `climate_systems_hurricane_vmax`, `climate_systems_thunder_velocity`, `climate_systems_mask_start`, `climate_systems_mask_height`, `climate_systems_square_spawns`, `climate_systems_track`, `climate_systems_thunder_square`. Every constant of the phase, `f_sys`'s included, is in the header (their one home).

**May be called by:** phases 07 to 10 (census, sim, Atlas, HUD), `run/src/climate/layer/`, the bench and the tests. Nothing in `run/src/game/` calls it yet.
**Depends on:** its parts `field/` (fronts and highs, for cells) and `name/`; the Owner (`climate_normals_at`, `climate_air_at`, `climate_mask_page_read`, `climate_mask_height_at`, the kind seeds and hooks, latitude), `climate/air` (mirror sign, wind latitude, sea temperature), `climate/season` (`climate_season_hurricane_s`), `terrain/rng`, `arena`, `assert`, `types`, libm.

Everything, with worked examples, is in `plans/004-ahmed-bohemoth-climate-system-phase-06-weather-systems.md`.
