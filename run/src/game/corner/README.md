# run/src/game/corner

**Purpose:** the HUD's top right corner while walking, plan 004 phase 10: the date line with the full season label of master plan 4.1, under it the climate line (the temperature at the body's own height, the wind's speed, the compass point it blows from and an arrow), and under that a storm warning on red. The sim works the numbers out and packs them into words; main turns the words into text for `ui_hud_corner_draw` (`run/src/ui/hud/`).
**Tier:** Arena by depth (2). It allocates nothing itself; the climate calls it makes take their lists from the game's tick arena.
**Owns:** nothing. `game_corner_t`, the words, is a line of the game's World arena beside the column jobs (`game_corner_of`, in `game/index.c`), as `game_t` is at its size and `app_t` at main's frame limit.
**Lifetime:** World. `game_corner_start` empties it as a world opens.
**Threads:** `game_hud_climate_publish` is the sim's (from `game_hud_publish` each tick), and whoever opens a world calls it once with `force` before the threads start (`game_open_climate_step`, as it finishes). `game_hud_corner_read` is main's. The four words are atomics, relaxed: only what is on the screen depends on them, each stays whole, and a frame that pairs a word a tick old with a new one is put right by the next. The rest of `game_corner_t` is the sim's alone.
**Public functions:**

- `game_corner_start`: nothing worked out.
- `game_hud_climate_publish`: every `GAME_CORNER_CLIMATE_TICKS` (20, a second), and at once with `force`, and on the first tick after the body comes back from death (a try that finds a tile not built yet waits for the next beat, not the next tick): `climate_weather_at` at the body's x and y at the blended ground (z NaN), `climate_normals_at` there for the ground's height and the cell's rain, `s_hur`, season and monsoon today; `T_HUD = T_now - 0.012 (z_feet - z_ground)` (master plan 6.6, `CLIMATE_LAPSE_C_PER_BLOCK`); the wind now; latitude and day length (phase 02); and `climate_season_of` (`climate/label`). Every `CLIMATE_WARNING_TICKS` (200, 10 s), on the first tick of a new census week, and on the same occasions: `climate_warning_at` at the body (`climate/warning`), on a worker; one that has never yet answered is tried again each second, not each tick. A value whose tile is not built yet keeps its last word; the season word's bit 16 stays clear until the first whole answer, and the climate line is not shown until then.
- `game_hud_corner_read`: line 0 empty (the settlement's, for a later plan); line 1 `Year 2 · Day 214 · Mid summer · Hurricane season` while `show_date` is on, with phase 01's season (`game_calendar_season_name`) until the sim's word is ready; line 2 the climate line while `climate_hud` has `SETTINGS_CLIMATE_HUD_LINE` and the words were ever worked out, with the arrow from the wind's direction and main's own camera yaw (so it turns as the player looks round between the sim's words), none below 0.5 blocks a second ("wind calm"); line 3 the warning while `SETTINGS_CLIMATE_HUD_WARNINGS` is on and there is one, on red.
- `game_hud_corners_draw`: main's, while walking, after a notice and before F3: the coordinates top left (if `show_coordinates` is on and F3 is not up), then the corner from `game_hud_corner_read`, clear of whichever top-left panel there is (`ui_hud_debug_panel` or `ui_hud_coordinates_panel`). It was in `game/index.c` until that file passed 1500 lines.
- `game_corner_wind_pack`, `game_corner_wind_unpack`: speed in tenths of a block a second in bits 0-15, the direction it blows toward in 65536ths of a turn anticlockwise from east in bits 16-31.
- `game_corner_compass`: the point of 8 it blows from, `atan2(-u, -v)` clockwise from north, N from 337.5 to 22.5. The trade wind of master plan 6.5, toward (-0.928, -0.371), is from E.
- `game_corner_arrow`: `alpha = toward - yaw`, `(-sin alpha, -cos alpha)` on the screen (x right, y down): up is the way the eye faces. Facing north with that trade wind, (-0.928, 0.371): left and a little down.
- `game_corner_climate_text`: `31.2 °C  wind 6.1 m/s from SW` or `-3.0 °C  wind calm`, in whole tenths, so nothing is ever `-0.0`. Blocks a second are metres a second (master plan 6.5).
- `game_corner_warning_pack`, `game_corner_warning_text`: the kind in bits 56-63; for a named storm its name seed in bits 0-31 and whether it is a hurricane now in bit 32, so main makes `Storm warning: Hurricane Tavela` (or `Tropical storm Tavela`) with `climate_system_name` and reads no climate; `Storm warning: hurricane likely`; `Storm warning: storm`.

**May be called by:** `game` (`game/index.c` for the layout, `game_hud_compose` and the world's opening; `game/tick` for the sim's publish), tests.
**Depends on:** `run/src/game/` (the game and its config), `run/src/climate/` and its parts `climate/label`, `climate/warning`, `climate/weather`, `climate/heat`, `climate/sun`, `climate/systems/name`; `run/src/calendar/`, `run/src/settings/persist/` (the bits), `run/src/ui/hud/` (the corner's plain values and its draw), `run/src/ui/` (the game's canvas and font), `types`, glibc's libm.
