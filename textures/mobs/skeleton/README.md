# textures/mobs/skeleton

The skeleton: bleached bone, dark sockets, ribs with gaps, and thin limbs of 2 by 12 by 2. A 64 by 32 skin.

Our own: painted by the recipe in `index.yaml`, from nothing but that recipe. It uses no pack's pixels, and owes no credit.
