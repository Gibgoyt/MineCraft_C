# Sound attribution

## Recordings

None. Every sound in this tree is made while building from a recipe in its own `index.yaml`, by our code in `compile/src/sound_pack/synth/`. No recording, sample or third-party data goes into them, so they owe no credit. No Minecraft sounds are used.

**Why there are no recordings:** Kenney's Impact Sounds was the first choice, but `https://kenney.nl/assets/impact-sounds` answered `403 Forbidden` to a plain download on 2026-11-05, so neither its files nor its licence were seen. See `plans/003-ahmed-bohemoth-initial-game-fixes-phase-05-sound.md`.

A recording added later gets a row here, and `compile/src/sound_pack/tree/` refuses one whose `origin` isn't written in this file:
